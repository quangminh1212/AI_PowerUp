## 🎯 Usage Guide

### 🎬 Creating Videos

1. **Choose Model** — Select an AI model (_Veo 2_, _Veo 3_, or _Veo 3 Fast_) depending on your needs: cinematic quality, balanced, or ultra-fast generation.  
2. **Upload Reference (Optional)** — Add one or more images to guide scene composition, characters, or style.  
3. **Enter Prompt** - Describe the video that you want to generate.   
4. **Set Aspect Ratio** — Choose from presets (16:9, 9:16) for platforms like YouTube, TikTok, or Instagram.  
5. **Check Credits & Cost** — Review the displayed estimate before generating.  
6. **Generate Video** — Click **Generate Video** to start. After processing, your result will be available in the preview area or via the notifications panel.  

### 🎬 Extend Video

1. **Select Last Frame From Video History** - Select the last frame from the existing video history as the starting point for the next video extension.
2. **Prompt** - Describe how you want to extend this video.
3. **Check Credits & Cost** — Review the displayed estimate before generating.  
4. **Extend Video** — Click **Extend Video** to start. After processing, your result will be available in the preview area or via the notifications panel.  

> [!NOTE]  
> Model, Aspect Ratio, and Resolution settings will be automatically set based on your selected video and cannot be modified.

### 💡 Tips for Best Results

- ✍️ **Be specific**: The more detailed your prompt, the better the result.
- 🖼️ **Use references**: Upload reference images to improve consistency.
- 📐 **Set aspect ratios**: Adjust image dimensions to match your use case (social media, banners, thumbnails).
